# Three-central-bank canary

## Intended request

> Compare the current policy rates of the U.S. Federal Reserve, European Central Bank and Bank of Canada using only their official websites. Give the latest decision date for each. You must use the live public web and include inline citations. If no web sources are retrieved, do not answer from memory; report that research failed.

This is the investigation's intended shared prompt. The historical Free-B request screenshot visibly preserves these words. Shared template files do not independently authenticate every historical submission.

## Why this task

The request reduces a large private research task to three named official sources and a small comparison. It preserves the requested live-web/citation contract while removing the private topic and most scope complexity. It is a reduction, not an unpredictable-fact experiment: the facts may be familiar or available through paths other than current-run retrieval.

## Expected contract

Under my requested contract, the system should provide a source-supported comparison with usable inline citations, or explicitly disclose inability to obtain the requested evidence. This describes the requested behavior, not a verified internal completion specification. A correct comparison has to name the actual instrument: the Federal Reserve sets a target range, and the ECB's key rates are not interchangeable. Rates change over time, so the historical outputs are not an answer key.

## Recording protocol

1. Start a new Chat Deep Research task. Record the exact submitted text and any plan edits or clarification responses; preserve the visible submitted request.
2. Record plan/account aliases, client/version, selected settings, reported network, and start/end time with timezone and time source. Distinguish observed settings from operator attribution. Retain identifiers privately.
3. Preserve the final status, displayed runtime/search/citation counters, sources/activity views, and the complete export. Distinguish `unknown` from a field the interface does not expose. If safe and available, capture the same run after reopening to compare representations.
4. Record whether citations are usable and whether the requested three-bank scope is covered. Separate generated source-use assertions from execution evidence.
5. Preserve originals and hashes before making separate sanitized derivatives. Keep diagnostics and identifying support material private. Record controls and all selected attempts, including explicit failures, without converting them into prevalence estimates.

These are recording recommendations for future use, not assertions that the retrospective dataset satisfies every step.

## Outcome interpretation

| Observed outcome | Classification and limit |
|---|---|
| Completed; both displayed counters zero; substantive source-specific report | Completed-0/0 observation. Retrieval state remains unresolved. |
| Nonzero displayed counters and usable citations | Operational provenance contrast; still requires separate scope and fact checking. |
| Explicit disclosure of inability to obtain evidence | Explicit failure under the requested contract; inspect any accompanying assertions separately. |
| Missing counters, interrupted task, or insufficient capture | Incomplete observation; record `unknown` or `not_exposed`, never an invented zero. |

A search counter need not capture direct page access. Zero citation telemetry does not imply absence of plain URLs. Matching known facts cannot prove live retrieval.
