Valid shared sections are not counted as omissions. Mentions of other programmes in shared material do not supply that separately requested research.

These are secondary completion-integrity observations. They do not establish a common cause with zero displayed provenance telemetry, and a missing section is not proof that no internal work occurred. No factual assessment of the programme advice is offered here.

Private originals are withheld because they contain applicant details and substantial unrelated research content. This public derivative does not independently expose every source line needed to verify every absence. Baseline reports that explicitly disclosed partial completion are a separate category; they are not relabeled as silent omissions.
